Why it's priced this way

The pricing follows a few rules

Free to find, free to book, free to train

Charging the people looking for a trainer would keep them away, and an empty marketplace helps no one.

Tools, not leads

Pay-per-lead pricing is resented by trainers and pushes them to move clients off the platform. A flat subscription with no take rate removes the reason to leave.

A free plan good enough to use

Profile, bookings, and payments work on the free plan. Pro adds leverage: more clients, more automation, no fee.

One price everywhere

Trainer subscriptions are sold on the web at the same price for everyone, and the iOS and Android apps recognize them.

Questions

Pricing questions

What does "0% platform fee" mean?

On Pro and Studio, Trainerdate takes nothing from the payments your clients make for sessions, packages, and memberships. You pay Stripe's card processing, which every payment app charges in some form.

What do I get as a founding trainer?

Three months of Pro free and a Founding Trainer badge that stays on your profile permanently. The offer is for trainers who join during the founding beta in the eight launch states.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from the billing portal at any time. Your profile, clients, and history stay put.

Do sales taxes apply?

Sales tax applies to the subscription where required. Some states also tax personal training services; trainers are responsible for taxes on their own services.

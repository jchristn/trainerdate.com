Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. The same document is in the Trainerdate app at app.trainerdate.com/privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lattice AI, LLC ("Lattice AI", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use Trainerdate: the website at trainerdate.com and app.trainerdate.com, the Trainerdate mobile apps for iOS and Android, and related services (together, the "Service"). Trainerdate helps people find personal trainers, gyms, studios, and academies, book and pay for sessions, follow training programs, and take part in a training community.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service. You can contact us at any time using the details in "Contact us" below.

Summary

  • We collect what you give us (account and profile details, messages, posts, bookings, training records), what your device sends when you use the Service, and limited information from sign-in, payment, and public web sources.
  • We use it to run the Service, keep it safe, and improve it. We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the Service shows no third-party ads.
  • Some features use artificial intelligence (AI) providers to process content on our behalf, for example to screen messages for abuse or to summarize reviews.
  • You can see, download, correct, and delete your information from your account settings, or ask us to do it.
  • The Service is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored only as a salted, slow hash), display name, handle, and date of birth (used to confirm you meet the minimum age).
  • Profile information: photo, biography, city and region, training interests and goals, disciplines and ranks, and other profile fields you choose to fill in. You control which profile fields are public, visible to members only, or private.
  • Trainer and business information: if you list yourself or a business, the details you provide, such as services, prices, availability, locations, photos, certifications and credential documents you upload for review, and payout details collected by our payment processor.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, forum threads, reactions, reviews, event listings, library lessons, photos, videos, documents, and community content.
  • Messages: the messages, photos, and files you send to other members, trainers, or businesses through the Service.
  • Bookings and payments: sessions you book or offer, packages and credits, attendance and check-ins, tips, refunds, and disputes. Card numbers and bank details are entered directly with our payment processor (Stripe) or the app store (Apple or Google); we receive only limited details, such as the card brand, last four digits, and payment status.
  • Training and fitness records: if you work with a trainer through the Service, the programs assigned to you, workouts you log, goals, body measurements, progress photos, intake forms, and waivers. See "Health and fitness information" below.
  • Support and feedback: what you send us in support requests, reports, ideas, and surveys.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage information: IP address, device type, operating system, app version, browser type, pages and screens viewed, features used, dates and times, and error and performance diagnostics.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device's precise location, used only while the app is open to show trainers, gyms, and events near you and to check you in. We do not collect location in the background. You can turn location access off in your device settings and search by city instead. We may also estimate a general area from your IP address.
  • Camera, photos, and calendar: only when you choose to use a feature that needs them, for example scanning a check-in code, uploading a photo, or adding sessions to your calendar.
  • Push notification tokens: if you allow notifications, an identifier that lets us send them to your device.
  • Cookies and local storage: the website uses strictly necessary cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences such as light or dark theme, and protect against abuse. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

Information from others

  • Sign-in providers: if you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook, we receive your name, email address (or an Apple private relay address), and an account identifier from that provider. We do not receive your password for that provider.
  • Payment processors and app stores: payment, subscription, payout, and dispute status from Stripe, Apple, and Google.
  • Other members: information other members provide about you, for example a trainer recording your attendance or progress, a member mentioning you, or a review of your business.
  • Public sources: to build our directory, we collect publicly available information about fitness businesses (such as gyms and studios) from public websites and open map data, such as business names, addresses, hours, and contact details, and may publish business listings. We may also collect publicly available professional information about individual trainers from the web to invite them to join; we do not publish public pages for individual trainers unless they create or claim one. If you want information about you or your business removed, use the "Remove my information" page or contact us.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide the Service: create and run your account, show your profile, connect members with trainers and businesses, deliver messages, handle bookings, payments, packages, and payouts, run communities, forums, events, and the training library, and send transactional emails and notifications.
  • Personalize the Service: show nearby results, recommend trainers, content, and events, and remember your settings.
  • Keep the Service safe: verify ages and credentials, detect and prevent fraud, spam, harassment, and abuse, enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and review reports.
  • Communicate with you: send account and security notices, booking and payment emails, responses to support requests, and, where you have not opted out, updates about the Service. You can change email and notification preferences in your account settings, and every optional email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Improve and develop the Service: understand how features are used, fix problems, and measure performance.
  • Comply with law and protect rights: meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, respond to lawful requests, and establish or defend legal claims.

Artificial intelligence features

Some features use AI models, including models provided by third-party AI providers that process data on our behalf. For example, AI may screen a first message from someone you have not talked to before, help moderate posts and reviews, summarize public reviews, suggest workout programs to a trainer, or answer questions in an assistant. We send these providers only the content needed for the task. Our agreements with them do not allow them to use your content to train their models. AI output can be wrong; trainers review program suggestions before they reach a client, and moderation decisions can be appealed by contacting us.

3. Health and fitness information

Workout logs, body measurements, progress photos, goals, injuries or limitations you mention in intake forms, and similar information can be health-related ("consumer health data" under some state laws, such as the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada law). We collect this information only when you choose to use training features or a trainer you work with records it, and we use it only to provide those features to you and the trainers you have connected with.

  • We share it only with the trainers and businesses you have connected with, and with service providers that process it on our behalf.
  • We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not collect it from your device's health platforms (such as Apple Health or Health Connect).
  • You can end a trainer connection at any time, which stops that trainer's access to new records, and you can delete your records or your account.
  • You can withdraw consent, access, or delete this information as described in "Your choices and rights" below.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • With other members and the public, as you choose: your public profile, public posts, reviews, forum threads, and public community content can be seen by others, including people who are not signed in and search engines. Members-only and private content is limited to the audience you select. Messages are shared with the people in the conversation.
  • With trainers and businesses you deal with: when you book, message, join, or connect with a trainer, gym, or community, they receive the information needed for that relationship, such as your name, contact details, booking details, attendance, and, if you connect for training, the training records described above.
  • With service providers: companies that process information on our behalf under contracts that limit their use of it, including cloud hosting and storage (Amazon Web Services, United States), email delivery (Mailgun), payments (Stripe), app stores and push notifications (Apple, Google, Expo), maps and geocoding (Amazon Location Service, OpenFreeMap), sign-in (Apple, Google, Meta), and AI processing (such as Anthropic).
  • For legal and safety reasons: to comply with law, a court order, or another legal process; to protect the rights, property, or safety of our members, the public, or us; and to investigate fraud or abuse.
  • In a business transfer: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent: in other cases when you ask us to or agree.

We do not sell personal information and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in California law. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

5. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as your account is open and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information, except:

  • conversations you were part of stay visible to the other participants until every participant has deleted them, after which they are purged within 30 days;
  • content that must be kept for legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, or safety reasons (for example payment records and records of moderation actions) is kept only as long as required; and
  • backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule.

Server request logs and diagnostics are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

From your account settings you can:

  • view and edit your profile and choose who sees each profile field;
  • download a copy of your information;
  • manage email, push, and in-app notification preferences;
  • turn off location, camera, and notification access in your device settings; and
  • delete your account (see the "Delete your account" page for the steps and what happens to your content).

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of your personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising, and certain profiling (we do not do these); to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and to withdraw consent. Residents of California, Washington, Oregon, Nevada, and other states with privacy laws have these rights under state law. You can exercise them in the app or by contacting us. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority. We will verify your request using information associated with your account, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

7. Security

We protect personal information with technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption of secrets and sensitive credentials at rest, slow salted password hashing, access controls, and audit logs of staff actions. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and you must be at least 18 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets a higher age). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States and process and store information in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, if the changes are material, notify you by email or in the Service before they take effect.

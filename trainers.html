Get found

Your profile is your website

Most independent trainers don't have a website. On Trainerdate you get a public profile built to rank for "personal trainer in {your neighborhood}" and "{your specialty} coach near me," plus a spot on the map, in matchmaking, and on city pages.

  • Everything a client checks before booking. Services and prices, real availability, gallery and intro video, specialties, philosophy, policies, and the gyms you train at.
  • Credibility on the page. Verified certification, insurance, and CPR badges, plus reviews only clients with a completed session can leave verified.
  • Help with the words. A profile writer interviews you and drafts your bio and service descriptions in your voice. You edit and publish.
  • Matched on fit. Matchmaking sends you people whose goals, schedule, and budget line up with how you train.
Fill the calendar

A booking link that works like a barber's

Barbers fill their chairs with a link clients can book from in three taps. Trainerdate brings that to training, with packages, recurring clients, and more than one location built in.

  • Your hours, your rules. Working hours per location, travel time between gyms, buffers, minimum notice, and how far ahead people can book.
  • Choose who books instantly. Instant book, request to book, or instant only for existing clients.
  • Cancellations refill themselves. When a slot opens, the waitlist is notified in order and the first to claim it gets it.
  • One calendar. Two-way Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 sync, so a dentist appointment blocks a slot automatically.
  • Share it anywhere. trainerdate.com/book/you, a QR code for business cards and gym flyers, and a "Book with me" widget for your own site.
The business side

Less chasing, more coaching

Every capability below exists to take an admin chore off your plate. Here is what each one gets you.

Payments and payouts

Stop sending Venmo reminders

Cards on file, deposits, automatic receipts, and tips that go 100% to you. Stripe pays out to your bank in about two business days and handles your 1099-K.

Packages and memberships

Get paid before the work, not after

Sell session packs with expiry dates, recurring monthly memberships, intro offers, and gift cards. Credits are used up at check-in, so the balance is always right.

No-show protection

Fewer empty hours

Reminders and confirmation requests go out automatically. Late-cancel and no-show fees you set are charged to the card on file with one tap, and clients see the policy before they book.

Intake and waivers

Paperwork done before the first session

Customizable intake questionnaires with a PAR-Q+ style health screen, plus liability waivers with e-signatures, stored per client and per version.

Trainer analytics

Know which numbers to move

Revenue, utilization, client retention, package burn, no-show rate, profile views, and how many inquiries turn into bookings. A weekly AI note points out the gaps worth filling.

Trainer-friendly gyms

Find a place to train clients

Gyms that welcome independent trainers post their terms: guest fee per session, monthly rent, insurance requirements. You find floor space without cold-calling front desks.

Coaching

Write a program once. Coach it all week.

The program builder has weeks, days, blocks, supersets, tempo, rest, and load as a weight, a percentage of 1RM, RPE, or RIR. Every exercise comes from a reviewed catalog with cues, common errors, and demo video.

  • Templates and progression rules. "Add 2.5 kg a week if every rep was completed" runs itself.
  • AI first drafts. From a client's goals, intake, equipment, and recent logs, AI drafts a program in your style. You edit it and approve it before the client sees anything.
  • Assign more than workouts. Habits like steps, protein, and sleep, weekly check-in forms, and library reading, each with due dates and reminders.
  • See what actually happened. Clients log sets, swap only to alternatives you approved, and send form-check videos you can comment on.
  • Walk in prepared. Before each session you get a summary: workouts completed, e1RM changes, soreness reported, sleep trends. Each line links to the record it came from.
Between sessions

An assistant that sounds like you and knows its limits

Clients of Pro trainers get an assistant grounded in their assigned program, your notes, the exercise catalog, and the rules you set. It answers the routine questions that would otherwise land in your texts at 9 pm.

  • Routine answers, from your plan. "What weight today?" "How do I do a Romanian deadlift?" "Can I swap lunges?" (only to swaps you approved).
  • Everything else comes to you. Pain, injury, medical questions, nutrition beyond general guidance, and program changes are passed to you as a message.
  • You see every conversation. And you can switch the assistant off for any client.

Questions

What trainers ask us

Do I own my client relationships?

Yes. Your clients are yours, and you can export your clients, programs, and history at any time.

Will Trainerdate take a cut of my sessions?

On Trainer Free, 5% of payments processed through the platform. On Pro and Studio, nothing: you pay card processing only. Tips are always 100% yours.

I work at two gyms and do home visits. Does that work?

Yes. Set working hours per location, add travel time between them, and mark services as in-gym, in-home, outdoor, or virtual. Clients only see slots that make sense for the location they pick.

I'm newly certified with no clients. Is this for me?

Yes. A credible profile with verified credentials, a free consult offer, and placement in matchmaking is the fastest way to a first client. The trainers-only business forum and library cover pricing, marketing, and insurance.

What's the Founding Trainer offer?

Trainers who join during the founding beta in the eight launch states get three months of Pro free and a Founding Trainer badge that stays on their profile permanently.

Will AI write to my clients without me?

No. Program drafts, posts, and profile copy wait for your approval. The between-sessions assistant answers only from your plan, sends anything sensitive to you, and you can see and switch off every conversation.

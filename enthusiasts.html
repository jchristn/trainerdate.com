Questions

Common questions

Does it cost anything to use?

No. Searching, booking, logging, progress tracking, and joining free communities are free. You pay coaches for their sessions and communities for paid tiers.

Can I log my own training without a coach?

Yes. The training log, catalog, and progress charts work on their own. If you start with a coach later, they see your history from day one (only if you share it).

Which disciplines are covered at launch?

Personal training, BJJ, MMA, yoga, and CrossFit-style training, with Pilates, boxing, Muay Thai, kettlebell, running, and the rest of the modality list following.

Is my data private?

Your profile and progress are private unless you choose to share them. Health questionnaires go only to trainers you book, and wearable data will require consent for each data type.

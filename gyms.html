How claiming works

Three steps from listing to lead channel

Unclaimed listings carry only business information: hours, address, website, disciplines, and the like. Nothing about individual staff is published until those people opt in.

  1. 1

    Find your listing

    Search the map or follow the "Is this your gym?" banner on your page. Missing entirely? Tell us and we'll add it.

  2. 2

    Verify you run it

    An owner or manager confirms control of the business, and the listing becomes Claimed. Corrections you make replace the sourced data.

  3. 3

    Fill it out

    Add photos, amenities, your class schedule, drop-in and day-pass prices, guest-trainer terms, and invite your trainers to link their profiles.

What a claimed listing does

Get found by people who are ready to train

Someone searching Trainerdate is already looking for a place or a person to train with. Each capability below puts your gym in front of them, or puts money in your pocket when they show up.

Map and search placement

Show up where people look

Your gym appears on the map, in filtered search, and on city and neighborhood pages built to rank in local search. Accurate hours and prices mean fewer "are you open?" calls.

Affiliated trainers

Your trainers' marketing becomes yours

Trainers who work at your gym link their profiles to your page. Every client who finds one of them sees your gym, and every visitor to your page sees who can train them there.

Trainer-friendly program

Earn rent from outside trainers

Post your guest-trainer terms (fee per session, monthly rent, insurance requirement) and independent trainers looking for space will find you. Empty floor time becomes income.

Classes and events

Fill classes with newcomers

List your class schedule, open mats, seminars, and workshops with capacity, waitlists, and QR check-in. Events appear on the map for travelers and people nearby.

Day passes and drop-ins

Sell to travelers before they arrive

Someone in town for three weeks finds your gym and buys a day pass or a class drop-in from their phone. Online sales for claimed gyms are On the roadmap

Reviews and analytics

See what's working and answer what isn't

Respond to reviews publicly, and track profile views, searches you appeared in, and leads, so you know whether the listing is earning its keep.

Questions

What gym owners ask us

How did my gym end up on Trainerdate?

Trainerdate maps gyms and studios in each launch city from public sources such as OpenStreetMap and the business's own website, respecting robots.txt and each source's terms. Every field records where it came from. Only business information is published.

Something on my listing is wrong. What do I do?

Claim it and fix it, or email us and we'll correct it. Listings are re-checked regularly for changed hours and closures, but the owner always has the final word.

Does claiming cost anything?

No. A claimed listing with your hours, photos, classes, affiliated trainers, and review responses is free. Studio is for gyms that want to run their team's scheduling and payments on Trainerdate.

Can I remove my gym?

Yes. Email us and we'll take care of it.

We're a BJJ academy, yoga studio, or box. Is this only for big-box gyms?

No. Academies, studios, and boxes are a core part of the launch, alongside personal training. Communities, rank and lineage, open mats, and seminars with tickets were built with those gyms in mind.
